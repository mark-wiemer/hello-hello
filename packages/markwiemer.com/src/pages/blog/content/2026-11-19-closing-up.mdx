postTitle: "Closing up"
postDate: "2026-11-19"
---

I was in the ER three times in October.
I've been on disability since then,
and I'll be on disability for the rest of the year.
And what a year it's been:

- January: DHS kills two American citizens, among many other crimes.
- February: I start interviewing for a new job.
- March: I get a new job, but I also get sick: that turns into pericarditis,
  which feels like a heart attack.
- April: I get sick again, start my new job, and end up with bronchitis.
  I also get a concussion and a neck sprain in a bus accident.
- May: On disability. My dog bites someone for the first time under my care.
- June: This was a good month! I returned to work and got a lot done.
- July: Boss and many others quit, I witness someone nearly die,
  I start seriously dating, I seriously question my religion,
  many other things happen, and I develop a plan to kill myself.
- August: On disability for mental health.
- September: I return to work part-time, then full-time,
  with my providers' approval.
  It goes OK, but honestly it's already foggy in my memory.
- October: Stress overwhelms me, and I end up in the ER
  with the worst pain I've ever experienced.
- November: On disability for holistic health.
- December: Hopefully my girlfriend follows through
  on our plan to meet each other's family!

This blog has been my favorite place to organize my thoughts.
I've loved the persistent nature of it:
the data is in several clouds and won't be lost any time soon.
Anyone can reference it to see who I've been and who I've become.
I know my girlfriend has enjoyed reading it to see my growth over the years.
Even I've enjoyed going back to it, re-reading my favorite articles
to remember that none of this is truly new to me.
I'm just re-learning the same lessons over and over again.
But I've been pressuring myself to write recently,
and that never goes well for a hobbyist with other commitments.

I'm always sad when I find an old blog
and the final post is something arbitrary.
Nobody ever plans on ending a blog, it seems. They just end.
I always hope the person behind the keyboard is doing great.
Often, I know they are, because I know them in real life!

But the writing I'm doing now is more personal than ever before,
and a public space just isn't appropriate for that.
If you know me, then I'm happy to share what I'm working on, of course.
If you've stumbled over here from who knows where, well,
you'll have to endure some radio silence for a long while.
Don't worry about me.
I still have an amazing support structure
that continues to grow stronger every week.
You may see some other activity from the accounts linked on my home page.
Or maybe I'll step away from Internet socialization altogether.

So, some final updates:

- My neck still hurts, but we're making progress.
- These new ER visits aren't any new chronic condition,
  from what we can see so far. Just a relapse from the rest of this year.
  And if they are something new, well, I'll deal with that too.
- I have no intention of playing GTA VI until Rockstar is cleared by the courts.
  I don't own a console, but I also highly disapprove of union-busting.
- I've stepped back from every volunteer commitment, including blogging,
  to focus on my health. It's going well.
- My family, my friends, and my dog are doing well.
  I'm glad I only have to worry about one person in my life right now.

I'll see you all on the flippity-flip 🤓
