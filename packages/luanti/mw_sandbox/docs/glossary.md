## Glossary

- **aabb3f** - axis-aligned bounding-box (3D float)
- **block** - ambiguous, could mean node, could mean map block
- **BS** - node size, in world-space side-length. B was previously short for "block" the meaning of block has changed since then.
- **map block** - analogous to a Minecraft chunk
- **node** - equivalent to a Minecraft block
- **facing (stairs)** - e.g. north-facing stairs have their "gap" (the missing quarter of the node) on the south. As such, upright north-facing stairs are stairs that you go up when facing north and moving forward.
